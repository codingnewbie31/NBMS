# Overview

The Nashat Beverages Management System (NBMS) is designed to become the central platform for managing the day-to-day operations of Nashat Beverages.

Rather than functioning as a general business management application, NBMS is built specifically around the workflows, priorities, and operational needs of the company.

The goal is to provide a fast, reliable, and intuitive system that helps users complete their daily work with minimal effort.

---

# Vision Statement

To build a modern internal management system that allows a small team to efficiently operate an entire beverage business from one centralized platform.

---

# Core Principles

- Simplicity over complexity.
- Speed over unnecessary features.
- Consistency across every screen.
- Clear information hierarchy.
- Easy to maintain and expand.

---

# Long-Term Vision

As the business grows, NBMS should continue to evolve by adding new modules and improving existing workflows without requiring major redesigns.

The application should remain scalable, maintainable, and easy to use while supporting future desktop and mobile experiences.

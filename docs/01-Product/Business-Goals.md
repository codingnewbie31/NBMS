> Status: Draft

---

# Overview

NBMS is designed to improve the efficiency, organization, and reliability of daily operations at Nashat Beverages. Every feature should contribute to making work faster, easier, and more accurate.

---

# Primary Goals

- Centralize business operations in one system.
- Reduce manual paperwork and repetitive tasks.
- Improve inventory accuracy.
- Track production efficiently.
- Simplify customer order management.
- Monitor deliveries in real time.
- Improve decision-making through clear reporting.

---

# User Experience Goals

The system should:

- Feel fast and responsive.
- Require minimal clicks to complete tasks.
- Present important information clearly.
- Maintain a consistent interface across all modules.
- Be easy to learn and use.

---

# Technical Goals

- Build a scalable MERN application.
- Maintain clean and readable code.
- Support future feature expansion.
- Prepare for mobile optimization in future versions.
- Follow a specification-first development process.
